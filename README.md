## 🎮 About the Game

**Fire Keeper** is a 2D action roguelite about surviving an increasingly dangerous dungeon, one encounter at a time.

Take control of a living cauldron and fight your way through waves of candle and lantern enemies. Collect Fire Fragments, discover powerful upgrades, defeat challenging bosses, and unlock new cauldrons that fundamentally change how you approach combat.

Each run offers opportunities to experiment with different playstyles, build combinations of abilities, and push further into the dungeon.

The project began as an attempt to capture the feeling of playing *Tower Fortress* by Nitrome, alongside inspiration from games such as *The Binding of Isaac* and *Hades*. These influences informed the combat feel, run-based progression, and overall presentation.

## ✨ Features

- **8 dungeon rooms** containing 24 encounters, with difficulty increasing as you progress.
- **2 unique boss fights**, each featuring distinct attack patterns and mechanics.
- **7 playable cauldrons**, each with its own combat identity and abilities.
- **Run-based upgrade system** with stackable effects, synergies, and build choices.
- **Permanent progression** through Fire Fragment collection and cauldron unlocks.
- **Multiple enemy types**, including melee and ranged candles and lanterns.
- **Workshop** for purchasing, browsing, and equipping cauldrons.
- **Boss encounters** featuring shields, projectile patterns, and summoned enemies.
- **Run summaries** showing your performance, rewards, equipped cauldron, and collected upgrades.
- **Audio settings** with adjustable master, music, and sound effect volumes.

## 🫕 Playable Cauldrons

Each cauldron changes how you approach combat.

| **Cauldron** | **Playstyle**                                       |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Hearth       | Balanced standard melee combat                      |
| Spiky        | Punishes enemies that hit you in melee              |
| Ranged       | Mouse-aimed projectile attacks with a reload system |
| Teal         | Sweeping fireball attacks                           |
| Shotgun      | Powerful, short-range burst attacks                 |
| Thinner      | Starts with additional dash charges                 |
| Broken       | High damage, but maximum health is limited          |

The Hearth Cauldron is available from the beginning. The other six can be unlocked using Fire Fragments earned during runs.

## 🔥 Gameplay & Progression

### The Dungeon

Fight through eight rooms, each containing multiple enemy encounters. The dungeon gradually introduces more dangerous combinations of enemies before testing your abilities against two unique bosses.

- **Room 4:** Candle Golem boss.
- **Room 8:** Lantern Warden boss.

### Run-Based Upgrades

After clearing rooms, choose from randomly offered upgrades to shape your current run. Upgrades can improve movement, damage, survivability, dash abilities, and weapon behaviour.

Some upgrades are specific to certain cauldrons, encouraging experimentation and different build strategies.

### Permanent Progression

Defeated enemies drop Fire Fragments. These can be collected and spent in the Workshop to unlock additional cauldrons.

Your unlocked cauldrons remain available between runs, giving you more ways to approach the dungeon.

## 🔁 Core Gameplay Loop

1. Choose a cauldron and enter the dungeon.
2. Fight enemy waves and survive each encounter.
3. Collect Fire Fragments from defeated enemies.
4. Choose upgrades to strengthen your current run.
5. Defeat bosses and progress through the dungeon.
6. Earn rewards, unlock cauldrons, and experiment with new playstyles.
7. Start another run and see how far you can go.

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Engine:** Godot 4.7.2
- **Rendering:** Compatibility renderer
- **Language:** GDScript
- **Target Platforms:** Desktop and Web
- **Persistence:** Local save data for progression and settings

## 🚀 Running the Project

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Install [Godot 4.7.2](https://godotengine.org/download/).
3. Open Godot's Project Manager.
4. Select **Import** and locate the `project.godot` file in the downloaded project folder.
5. Open the project and press Play.

No additional plugins or external dependencies are required to run the base project in Godot.

## 🎨 Development & AI Usage

Fire Keeper was designed and directed by me. I developed the initial enemy concepts, visual designs, gameplay ideas, and overall creative direction.

My primary experience is in game art and design rather than programming. To take the project beyond static concepts and turn it into a playable prototype, I used generative AI tools, primarily ChatGPT, as a programming assistant.

AI helped me write and debug GDScript, implement gameplay systems, and iterate on mechanics. I provided the design direction, defined how the systems should behave, tested the implementation, reviewed the results, and made the decisions about what to keep, change, or discard.

The intention was to use AI as a tool to bridge the gap between my design ideas and my limited programming experience, allowing me to build and demonstrate a playable game rather than only present a design document.

All creative direction and final design decisions remained mine.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of Fire Keeper was to take an initial game concept and develop it into a functional, playable prototype.

The project explores action combat, enemy behaviour, boss encounter design, player choice, run-based upgrades, permanent progression, and replayability.

It also served as an opportunity to explore how an artist and designer can use AI-assisted development to bring a game concept to life.
